A make.fun bounty entry: one word — the single word a grandma would text
about AI.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) scores seven candidate words against
the judge brief's five criteria in the brief's own order (weights 5/4/3/2/1)
and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

The winning word: **witchcraft** — grandma's verdict on AI. Warm, a little
baffled, funny in one word, and tied to AI as her reaction to it.
